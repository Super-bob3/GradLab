# Gradient Backgrounds for Social Media

A well-designed gradient background makes social content stand out in a feed. GradLab produces high-resolution gradient images and videos ready to use across every major platform.

## Platform dimensions

Set the canvas size in GradLab to match your target platform before exporting:

| Platform | Format | Dimensions |
|----------|--------|-----------|
| Instagram post | Square | 1080 × 1080 px |
| Instagram Story / Reels | Portrait | 1080 × 1920 px |
| X (Twitter) post image | Landscape | 1200 × 675 px |
| X header | Banner | 1500 × 500 px |
| LinkedIn post | Landscape | 1200 × 628 px |
| LinkedIn banner | Banner | 1584 × 396 px |
| YouTube thumbnail | Landscape | 1280 × 720 px |
| YouTube channel art | Banner | 2560 × 1440 px |
| Facebook cover | Banner | 820 × 312 px |

Enter these values in the **Canvas** W and H fields, then click **Download Frame** to export a PNG at the correct size.

## Tips for social content

### Keep colors on-brand

Use the **Upload Image** feature to extract a color palette from your logo, a campaign photo, or an existing brand asset. This ensures the gradient matches your visual identity.

### Use film grain for a premium feel

Enable **Post-Processing → Film Grain** at a low value (0.1–0.2). The subtle texture makes the gradient feel less digital and more intentional.

### Animate for Stories and Reels

For video content:
1. Set canvas to 1080 × 1920 for portrait
2. Enable **Ping-pong Loop**
3. Click **Record MP4**, record 3–6 seconds, stop
4. Import the video into your editing tool (CapCut, Premiere, Final Cut) as a background layer

### Add text overlay

Export the gradient as a background layer, then add your text, product image, or overlay in a design tool (Figma, Canva, Photoshop). The gradient provides the background; your content sits on top.

### Create a cohesive series

Design one gradient and export it at multiple dimensions for different platforms. The same palette at 1:1, 9:16, and 16:9 creates a consistent visual identity across formats.

## Quick export workflow

1. Open [GradLab](https://gradlab.app)
2. Set canvas dimensions for your target platform
3. Pick your colors and adjust the animation
4. Click **Download Frame** for a static PNG
5. Import into your design or content tool
