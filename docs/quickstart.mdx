## Open GradLab

Navigate to [gradlab.app](https://gradlab.app) in any modern browser. The canvas begins rendering immediately using one of three built-in presets. No sign-up required.

<Note>
  GradLab requires WebGL support. Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge all support WebGL by default on desktop and mobile.
</Note>

## Step 1 — Choose a Preset

Three presets appear at the top of the control panel. Click any to load a full configuration — colors, flow algorithm, and post-processing included.

Use presets as a starting point, not a final result. Every parameter is editable after loading.

## Step 2 — Set Your Colors

In the **Palette** section, click any color swatch to open the color picker. You can:

- Type a hex code directly
- Drag swatches to reorder the blend sequence
- Click the image icon to extract colors from a photo
- Add up to 8 colors, remove down to 2

Colors are blended in OKLab space by default, producing smooth, perceptually uniform transitions.

## Step 3 — Pick a Flow Algorithm

Open **Shape & Flow** and choose one of 13 algorithms from the dropdown. Each algorithm defines the underlying motion structure of the gradient:

- **Sin/Cos (Classic)** — smooth periodic waves, a reliable starting point
- **Fluid Dynamics (Chaos)** — turbulent, organic motion
- **Radial Topography (Rings)** — concentric rings radiating from center
- **Nested SDF Shape (Core Glow)** — geometric glow emanating from a signed-distance field shape

Use the **Speed**, **Zoom**, **Liquid**, and **Morph** sliders to fine-tune the motion feel.

## Step 4 — Adjust Post-Processing (Optional)

In the **Post-Processing** panel you can add:

- **Film Grain** — analog texture over the gradient
- **Inner Border** — a soft or hard vignette frame
- **3D Tilt** — a subtle perspective warp that responds to mouse movement

## Step 5 — Export

When the canvas looks right, choose your export format:

| Button | Output |
|--------|--------|
| **Download Frame** | PNG screenshot at full resolution |
| **Record MP4** | Click to start, click to stop — exports MP4 or WebM |
| **Export Code** | Self-contained HTML file you can embed anywhere |

<Tip>
  Enable **Ping-pong Loop** before recording to get a seamlessly looping video — perfect for social media or UI backgrounds.
</Tip>
