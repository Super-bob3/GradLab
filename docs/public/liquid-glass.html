Shape & Flow

13 GPU-shaded fluid algorithms — from sine waves to curl noise vortices and signed-distance field shapes. Every algorithm runs as a GLSL fragment shader on the GPU.

Palette & OKLab

Up to 8 colors blended in OKLab perceptual space. Drag to reorder. Extract a palette from any photo. Color transitions are perceptually uniform — no muddy intermediates.

Post-Processing

Non-destructive compositing passes layered over the gradient. Film grain, inner border, corner radius, 3D parallax tilt. All computed on the GPU with zero performance cost.

Export Anywhere

PNG at 2× minimum resolution, MP4/WebM with ping-pong loop, or a self-contained HTML file with zero dependencies. Drop into an iframe with no build step.

ASCII Matrix

Full-canvas character grid that reacts to mouse proximity in real time. 7 character sets plus custom text input. Heat-map decay creates organic ripple effects.

Free & Open
